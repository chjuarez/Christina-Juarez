    A straight conversation about your property, your timing, and what you want out of the sale. Ask me anything.

FAQ

Fair questions, straight answers.

What happens on a Seller Call?

We talk through your property, your timing, and what you want from the sale. You ask whatever you want. If it makes sense to keep going, the next step is a walk of the property. If it doesn’t, no hard feelings.

Do I need to fix up my place before I call?

No. Call first. The Walk + Prep Plan exists so you know what’s worth doing and what’s worth skipping before you spend anything.

Who will I actually be working with?

Me. I’m a solo broker, so the person you talk to on the Seller Call is the person who walks your property, negotiates your sale, and sends your Sunday update.

What is the Sunday update?

Every Sunday while your property is listed, you hear from me: what happened that week and what’s next. You never have to chase me for news.

What areas do you cover?

Valley Center and Pauma Valley. I live in Valley Center and I keep my work close to home.

What’s different about selling a country property?

Country properties come with questions a tract home doesn’t: wells and water, septic, acreage and usable land, access roads, outbuildings, zoning. Buyers will ask about all of it, so we get the answers ready before launch.

I’m looking for land. Where do I start?

Start with what you want the land to do. Then we look at what decides whether it can: water, access, zoning, utilities, and topography. Call or text me and tell me what you have in mind.

How do the Country Property Files work?

Each file leads with the real address, the price, and the specs. I describe what a property offers, and you decide whether it fits.
